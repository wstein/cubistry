The corresponding full-face files are F.svg, B.svg, R.svg, L.svg, U.svg, D.svg.
Each face uses a 320 by 320 viewBox. Quadrant files use a 160 by 160 viewBox
with its original face offset; the physical source sticker supplies its frame.
