Names (long chain, print D, January 1980 - January 2036)
Long chain from the solved cube (January 1980, "Magic Date") to January 2036: 673 states, 672 edges, 76262 HTM, 90379 QTM. Six best-effort months (1981-02, 1987-02, 1998-02, 2009-02, 2015-02, 2026-02): the front differs from its display in row 7, column 1 (the red 29).
Status: research candidate, not certified, not for release. Print D (print C relabeled to the root January 1980) is not frozen. The best-effort edges are not confirmed as exact by the checkers. No shortest-route, global-print or build-certification claim.
