    Goals, keyboard controls & supported puzzles

    Exact means all eight physical pieces and 24 sticker frames match the original target, even in classic colors. Rotational equivalence allows any of the 24 whole-cube orientations. Rotating the presentation changes neither goal.

    Keyboard: focus a face button and press Enter or Space, or type a legal word and press Enter. Away from inputs, R L U D F B turns a face; Shift adds inverse. Escape cancels after the active legal turn.

    Scrambles are repeatable recorded legal words, not uniform random states. Solve uses the pinned PuzlWerk 2×2 capability and verifies the full route before playback and the endpoint afterward. Exact routes are not guaranteed optimal.

    3×3: Unsupported in this playground. Its shared interactive mechanics and viewer profile are 2×2 only. Cubistry’s Calendar uses a separate 3×3 picture provider; this is not a claim about that provider’s capabilities. No uploads, larger puzzles, or saved sessions.
