H/M/S Counter

Counts one step per second, wrapping through 00. Upper-left stays blank. Settled number changes take at most six moves. A new target replaces old work after the current turn. Changing the unit sets up a fresh 00 display.

M/S Counter

Counts one step per second, wrapping through 00. Upper-left stays blank. Every +1 plays a saved step of at most four moves and every −1 plays the previous step backward. On this print (S3) seconds stay within three moves; a print with both units within three is unknown. Set and Reset walk the saved steps the shorter way round. Changing the unit sets up a fresh 00 display.

1/10-Second Counter

Counts one tenth of a second per step, wrapping through 0..9. The 1/10 unit sticker at upper-right stays stationary while the single Period-1 closed sequence plays on the lower-left digit at 50 ms per QTM. Digit buttons and step increments walk the saved period edges turn by turn.
