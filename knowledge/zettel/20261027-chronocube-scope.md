# Chronocube — prioritize birthdays and 1900–2099

The primary use case is displaying personal dates, particularly birthdays, together with time and weekday where relevant. For the first implementation, target **1900–2099** (20th and 21st centuries). This is a product scope goal, not a new mathematical proof.

Keep larger-century feasibility experiments separate: successful research does not automatically expand the product requirements. Optional blank millennium symbols and deeper historical coverage are **not MVP requirements**.

Related: [Historical calendar as a separate application](20261027-historical-calendar.md), [Century research stays exploratory](20261027-century-research-boundary.md).
