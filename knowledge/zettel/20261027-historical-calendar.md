# A historical calendar as a separate Cubistry application

A future historical or commemorative cube could prioritize day, month and year across broad historical periods, including years before the common era. Exact clock time would often be unnecessary; weekday may depend on the historical calendar convention.

Treat this as a **separate application of the shared Cubistry engine**, not a Chronocube feature request. No implementation or specification is authorized by this idea.
