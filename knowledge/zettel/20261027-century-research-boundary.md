# Extended-century proofs are research, not scope

Continue ongoing mathematical experiments about century digits and alternate layouts on the dedicated research branch. Preserve exact assumptions, proof provenance, and SAT/UNSAT/UNKNOWN distinctions.

Do not let optimization for maximum historical coverage block or redesign the initial Chronocube, whose implementation target is **1900–2099**. Extra millennia, optional blank digits and a future historical cube are separate possibilities rather than accepted requirements.

Related: [Chronocube scope](20261027-chronocube-scope.md), [Historical calendar](20261027-historical-calendar.md).
