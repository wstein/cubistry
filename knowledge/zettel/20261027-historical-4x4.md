# A 4×4 cube for historical dates

A 4×4×4 cube has no single fixed face-center sticker, which could give a historical year display more layout flexibility than the present 5×5 design. However, it has fewer sticker faces (96 versus 150) and legal cubie permutations remain constrained.

The tradeoff between mobile centers and total capacity is an **open feasibility question**, not a proof or a chosen design. A later research spike could compare 4×4, 5×5 and 6×6 if the separate historical application is pursued.
