# A duplicated physical title makes the 2×2 counter feasible

A single immutable 24-sticker print can show all 100 two-digit values beneath
two constant upright title images on a 2×2 front. Each title fragment is printed
twice and its physical cubies may move. A separate fixed print replaces the
tens-zero image with a blank for unpadded values. Both are **SAT**, established
by complete legal words and an independent labeled-facelet replayer.

Requiring the same two top cubies to remain fixed is **UNSAT**: the remaining
six corners provide 18 image positions, while either numeric format requires
20. Constant completed text does not imply constant physical cubies.

The [complete proof and reproduction](../../docs/COUNTER_FEASIBILITY.md) link to
the frozen printing and exact move words. This supports the
[two-digit counter concept](20261027-two-digit-cube-counter.md) without changing
its size or silently substituting an external text overlay. Glyphs and title
fragments remain rigidly attached to their source stickers during all turns.
