These six whole-face raster illustrations (two women at a cafe table, a rowing boat on a lake at sunset, a wren on a blossoming fence, a sleeping dog by a rainy window, a shepherd with sheep and a dog, and a bench under a tree) are a work separate from the
Cubistry program code, which stays under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The images are
licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; the AGPL copyleft does not cover them.

The WebP files are lossy copies of the six original PNG files (the larger two are
scaled to 1024 px). The playground cuts each face into the N by N stickers a cube of
size N needs when it loads them, so no pre-cut tiles are stored. To re-pin the faces
after a change, run: node scripts/build-playground-theme.mjs frieden
