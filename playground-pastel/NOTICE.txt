These six original vector illustrations and their 24 cropped SVG sticker tiles
are a work separate from the Cubistry program code, which stays under
AGPL-3.0-or-later. The images are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; the AGPL copyleft
does not cover them.
The six characters (Pip, Nimbo, Tulia, Moss, Sunny, Plop) are original inventions
and are not modeled on any licensed character. The SVG files in this directory
contain no text and no fonts; no third-party images, platform glyphs, or stock
artwork are included.

Editable source and deterministic regeneration:
  scripts/build-pastel-garden-art.mjs
  node scripts/build-pastel-garden-art.mjs

The art generator is the editable source of the artwork, so it is art data under
the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license, not AGPL program code. The unused Up/Down draft
proposals in assets/playground-pastel-proposals/ are part of the same artwork
and carry the same license.
