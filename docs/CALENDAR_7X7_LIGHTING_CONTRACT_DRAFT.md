Washed share counts eligible non-banner tiles with a token channel at most 235 whose sampled fill has all channels at least 253. Clipped share counts sampled light pixels (luma above 150) whose channels are all at least 253. The yaw metric is the maximum absolute change in mean sampled light-pixel luma between adjacent visible-face samples. Fill deviation is the median across front tiles of the largest absolute 8-bit sRGB channel difference from each tile's source token. The head-on color probe separately uses the names window print `public/calendar7/names/state.v1.json` (SHA-256 `2993ee875add87d8b6feedd5e199e9e6d907ed0b32bd9f961f953a0b37a373c7`). The existing probes define sampling and visibility; neither the print source nor the sampling area changes.

The proposed exceptions apply only at the named scheme and view, except the yaw limit which applies to the existing Rubik-Tint sweep. Other views retain washed share 0.25 and Rubik-Tint clipped share 0.30 and top-minus-front 0.15. Front washed share 0, front p90 9, top median deviation 35, pitch step 20, per-tile fill 7, ink and line 6, and the other scheme and effect-mode medians remain unchanged. The exceptional Solarized Light result is a substantial loss of color; its small numerical margin does not make it visually equivalent to the prior requirement. The very small measured margins need independent platform and CI validation before deciding whether they are practical. No production light, exposure, material or token is changed by this proposal.
