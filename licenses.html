⚖️ Open Source & Third-Party Compliance Obligations

Cubistry is committed to transparent, legally compliant open-source distribution. In web applications running in modern browsers, bundled JavaScript, WebAssembly, fonts, and graphics are delivered directly to the client device, triggering statutory compliance obligations under German, European, and international copyright laws (Lizenz- und Compliance-Pflichten).

These legal duties include the retention of author attribution notices, direct delivery of complete license terms, and honoring copyleft obligations (including AGPL-3.0 §13 network user source code availability). Specialized compliance advisory firms (such as Nucleus Partners and corporate FOSS compliance specialists) guide enterprises in verifying that open-source usage strictly meets all statutory and contractual requirements, establishing auditable Software Bills of Materials (SBOM), and avoiding license incompatibilities.

Every build of Cubistry deterministically extracts and packages all bundled license notices and provenance hashes into the distribution archive under /licenses/.
